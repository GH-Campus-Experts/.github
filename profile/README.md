## 🏛️ About the Organization
The **RISU Tech Ecosystem** is an elite, student-driven initiative officially recognized by Rungta International Skills University. Our mandate is to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry-grade software engineering through open-source contributions, real-world project development, and rigorous technical bootcamps.

---

## 🌐 Domains of Excellence
We focus on cultivating expertise across cutting-edge technological domains:

| 🧠 AI & Data Science | ☁️ Cloud & DevOps | 💻 Software Engineering |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Deep Learning, NLP | Docker, Kubernetes, AWS | Full-Stack Architecture |
| Open-Source AI Models| CI/CD Pipelines | Scalable System Design |
| Data Analytics | Serverless Computing | Web3 & Blockchain |

---

## 🚀 Strategic Initiatives
- **Open-Source Incubation:** Mentoring students to contribute to major global organizations (e.g., CNCF, Apache, Mozilla).
- **Industry-Aligned Bootcamps:** Conducting professional masterclasses on modern frameworks and version control methodologies.
- **Research & Development (R&D):** Building in-house technological solutions for the university and the broader community.
- **Hackathons & Sprints:** Hosting high-stakes, time-bound coding competitions to solve real-world problems.
